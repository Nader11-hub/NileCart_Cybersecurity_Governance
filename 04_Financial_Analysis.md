## 1. Methodology & Valuation Basis (Estimated Figures)
All financial figures, asset valuations, and loss expectations are **estimated coursework projections** based on typical small-to-medium e-commerce operations in Cairo, Egypt. No proprietary or real financial data from NileCart Egypt is used.

### Valuation Parameters & Basis
- **Single Loss Expectancy (SLE):** The estimated monetary loss resulting from a single successful realization of a risk.
- **Annualized Rate of Occurrence (ARO / p):** Estimated probability or frequency of the threat occurring per year.
- **Annualized Loss Expectancy (ATLE):** $\text{SLE} \times p$.

---

## 2. Risk R1: Credential Compromise & Unauthorized Access (Critical)
- **Exposed Asset Value (Base):** Estimated EGP 120,000 (representing customer PII exposure, breach notification overhead, and potential regulatory inquiry costs).
- **Exposure Factor (EF):** Estimated 25% ($0.25$).
- **Single Loss Expectancy (SLE):** $\text{EGP } 120,000 \times 0.25 = \mathbf{\text{EGP } 30,000}$.
- **Annualized Rate of Occurrence ($p$):** Estimated $0.5$ (once every two years given lack of MFA).
- **Annualized Loss Expectancy (ATLE):** $\text{EGP } 30,000 \times 0.5 = \mathbf{\text{EGP } 15,000\text{/yr}}$ (Estimated).

## 3. Risk R6: Backup Failure & Ransomware Outage (High)
- **Exposed Asset Value (Base):** Estimated EGP 80,000 (representing inventory database downtime, lost revenue during outage, and recovery labor).
- **Exposure Factor (EF):** Estimated 30% ($0.30$).
- **Single Loss Expectancy (SLE):** $\text{EGP } 80,000 \times 0.30 = \mathbf{\text{EGP } 24,000}$.
- **Annualized Rate of Occurrence ($p$):** Estimated $0.4$ (40% chance per year without isolated/tested backups).
- **Annualized Loss Expectancy (ATLE):** $\text{EGP } 24,000 \times 0.4 = \mathbf{\text{EGP } 9,600\text{/yr}}$ (Estimated).
